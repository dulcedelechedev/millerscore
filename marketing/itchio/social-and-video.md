# Launch communications

## Short post

MillerScore connects music notation, piano-roll performance editing, virtual
instruments, and audio tracks in one project. Windows Early Access is coming
with SoundFonts, VST3, ASIO recording, and GPLv3 source. Developed by
**DulceDeLecheDEV** for musicians who compose from the score and produce the
details of the performance.

## Community post

I am developing MillerScore, an independent GPLv3 fork of MuseScore Studio
designed to bring notation and music production closer together. The DAW view
uses the instruments and notes from the score itself, with piano-roll editing,
performance adjustments, SoundFonts/VST3, and synchronized audio tracks.

The project is in Early Access. CC0–127 can already be selected, edited, and
saved, but generic playback for every controller is still in development.
Current recording uses ASIO and does not yet include input selection,
monitoring, or input-latency compensation.

I am especially looking for feedback from composers, arrangers, educators, and
score-first producers. Repository and corresponding source:
https://github.com/dulcedelechedev/millerscore
