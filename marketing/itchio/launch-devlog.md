# MillerScore enters Early Access: notation and production in one project

Today I am introducing the first MillerScore Early Access release: an open
music workstation for people who think in notation and also need detailed
control over performance, instruments, and audio.

The central idea is simple: the score remains the musical source of the
project. Switch to the DAW view and its instruments appear as tracks, the
playhead follows the same playback, and the notes appear in the piano roll.
Instead of exporting MIDI and losing the connection to the written music, you
can adjust velocity, timing, and sounding duration while preserving notation.

This release also brings together SoundFonts, compatible VST3 instruments,
WAV/MP3/FLAC import, and the first phase of ASIO recording. The Black & Gold
interface gives the project a consistent identity across notation and
production surfaces.

## Why Early Access?

Important work remains. Generic playback for every MIDI CC is not complete,
recording still needs input selection, monitoring, and input-latency
compensation, and the test matrix needs to grow. These limits are stated
clearly so development can benefit from informed, real-world feedback.
